About the project

Scheduling software shaped around real planning pressure.

Roster Burrito Local is the lightweight desktop edition of a broader scheduling platform: narrower in scope, faster to start, and focused on local roster building.

Why local first

A smaller product surface keeps the planning workflow easier to trust.

The local edition keeps sensitive planning data in local project files and gives a single admin the essential tools to build, review, and export schedules. The hosted product can focus on collaboration, accounts, and governance as a separate workflow.
